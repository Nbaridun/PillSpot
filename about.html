About PillSpot

Making medicine identification simple, accessible, and convenient through modern technology.

Who We Are

PillSpot is a medicine identification website designed to help users identify medicines by uploading an image of a medicine strip. Our platform uses image analysis technology to provide useful information about medicines.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make medicine information easier to access and understand through a simple and user-friendly digital platform.

Safety First

PillSpot is an informational tool and should not replace professional medical advice. Identification results may not always be accurate. Always verify medicine information with the packaging, a pharmacist, or a qualified healthcare professional before taking any medicine.
